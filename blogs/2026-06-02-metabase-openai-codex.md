We’re excited to announce that Metabase is now available as a plugin in ChatGPT and Codex, part of OpenAI’s new data analytics launch. You can now connect your Metabase instance directly to Codex, ask questions, find insights, save questions, and dashboards. The tables and metrics curated in your Metabase’s semantic layer are now available for you to inspect, find insights, and do data analyses using OpenAI’s Codex.
