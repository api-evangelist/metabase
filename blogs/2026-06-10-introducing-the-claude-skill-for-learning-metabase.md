We’ve built a Claude skill that teaches you Metabase . It keeps track of what it has already covered, uses your own data for examples, and is designed for learning not just one-off questions. Why use AI for learning Metabase Compared to static guides, an LLM can be much more engaging, ask you questions, and then use your answers to decide what else to cover or where to maybe add some information.
